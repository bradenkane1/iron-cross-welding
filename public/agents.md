# Iron Cross Agent Guide

## Purpose

This file is for AI agents, assistants, agentic browsers, and language models that need to understand Iron Cross Welding & Fabrication, find the right section of this website, and help a person contact the business without overstepping.

This website is the canonical source for what Iron Cross does, where it works, its hours, its certifications, and how to request a quote. If this file and the live page disagree, use the live page.

There is no online store, cart, or checkout. Do not look for Shopify, product prices, or a payment flow. The commerce actions on this site are a phone call, a text, or an email draft the person sends themselves.

## Agent Rules

1. Use this website for services, hours, certifications, and contact details. Do not invent an address, a price, a warranty, or a certification that is not written here.
2. Do not say a quote was submitted, saved, or sent. The form only opens an email draft.
3. Ask the person before calling, texting, or opening that draft. Do not place the call or send the email yourself.
4. For an emergency or a same-day field repair, tell them to call (949) 329-3012.
5. If a job is steel and it is not listed, say the business will tell them straight whether they can do it. Do not promise that they will.

## For Assistants Acting on Behalf of a Person

Help the person describe the job. Useful details are the type of work, dimensions, material, timeframe, and where a mobile rig should go. Job types on the form are:

- Fences and gates
- Truck, flatbed, or trailer
- Heavy or farm equipment
- Mobile on-site repair
- Railings, handrails, and stairs
- Custom fabrication
- Something else

**Calling or texting.** Confirm with the person first. The number is (949) 329-3012. A text uses the same number.

**Quote form.** Submitting it does not send email and does not store the request. It opens the person's email app with a draft to theironcrosswelding@gmail.com. The subject is "Quote request:" plus the job type. The body includes name, phone, email, type of job, and project details. The person has to review that draft and send it.

**Photos.** The file field does not attach photos. A mailto link cannot carry files. If photos were chosen, only their names are written into the draft. Tell the person to attach the photos in their email app before they send.

**Emergencies.** Do not wait on the form. Have the person call.
